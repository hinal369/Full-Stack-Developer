Overview

Paneer Lababdar is a rich, creamy, and mildly spicy North Indian curry. It features soft paneer cubes simmered in a luscious gravy made from tomatoes, cashews, and aromatic spices. This restaurant-style recipe takes about 40 minutes to make and yields 4 to 5 servings.

Ingredients

For the Gravy:

  • 4 medium tomatoes (roughly chopped)
  • 10–12 cashews
  • 1 inch ginger (chopped)
  • 3–4 garlic cloves
  • Whole spices: 1 green cardamom, 2 cloves, and 1 bay leaf

For the Main Curry:

  • 250g paneer, cut into 1-inch cubes
  • 50g paneer, finely grated (adds thickness)
  • 1 large onion, finely chopped
  • 2 tbsp butter or oil (plus 1 extra tbsp butter for finishing)
  • 1/2 tsp cumin powder
  • 1 tsp Kashmiri red chili powder (for color)
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • 1/4 tsp garam masala
  • 1 tsp Kasuri methi (dry fenugreek leaves), crushed
  • 1/4 tbsp heavy cream or low-fat creamr 1/2
  • tsp sugar
  • Salt to taste
  • 1/2 cup water (plus more for boiling)

Instructions

Follow these steps to prepare the dish:

  1. Heat oil in a pan and add the whole spices.
  2. Add the chopped onions and cook until golden brown.
  3. Add the ginger-garlic paste and cook for a minute.
  4. Add the tomatoes and cook until they soften.
  5. Add the cashews and cook for a few minutes.
  6. Add the spices and cook for a minute.
  7. Add the paneer cubes and cook for a few minutes.
  8. Add the cream and sugar, and cook for a minute.
  9. Season with salt and garnish with fresh coriander.
