Open Graph Example

What is Open Graph?

Open Graph controls how your webpage appears when shared on social media platforms.

This page includes:

  • og:title
  • og:description
  • og:image
  • og:url

How to test it?

Upload this page online and share its URL on:

  • Facebook
  • LinkedIn
  • WhatsApp
  • Discord

You will see a rich preview card with image, title, and description.
